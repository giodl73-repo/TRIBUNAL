# TRIBUNAL role index

Roles return pass, finding, or defer. Critical and major findings block promotion. Dissent remains recorded.

## Parliament

- [justice-system-planner](parliament/justice-system-planner.md) — Coordinates caseflow without replacing independent decisions.
- [judge-due-process-steward](parliament/judge-due-process-steward.md) — Protects judicial independence and fair process.
- [public-defender-legal-aid-advocate](parliament/public-defender-legal-aid-advocate.md) — Protects counsel, liberty, access, and contestability.
- [prosecutor-public-safety-lead](parliament/prosecutor-public-safety-lead.md) — Protects lawful safety and evidence integrity.
- [corrections-reintegration-practitioner](parliament/corrections-reintegration-practitioner.md) — Tests detention, supervision, release, and reentry handoffs.
- [victim-services-advocate](parliament/victim-services-advocate.md) — Protects notice, safety, voice, and recovery.
- [civil-rights-community-trust-advocate](parliament/civil-rights-community-trust-advocate.md) — Tests disparate effects, legitimacy, and equal protection.
- [justice-operations-finance-analyst](parliament/justice-operations-finance-analyst.md) — Checks workload, capacity, cost, and non-additivity.
