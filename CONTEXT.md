# TRIBUNAL context

TRIBUNAL is the Justice 2.0 domain owner for Taxlane's JUS track. It examines
timeliness and resolution together with counsel, liberty, victim notice,
equality, evidence integrity, and contestability.

Current flagship: a rights-bounded aggregate caseflow screen. It is research
tooling, not legal advice, a risk score, sentencing, detention, prosecution,
adjudication, or operational direction.
