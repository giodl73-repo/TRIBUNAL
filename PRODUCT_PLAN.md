# TRIBUNAL product plan

## Outcome

Help analysts test whether justice-system changes improve timeliness and
resolution without degrading counsel, liberty, victim protection, equality,
independence, evidence integrity, or contestability.

## Product-first sequence

1. Rights-bounded caseflow screen — implemented.
2. Official aggregate caseload and interval source pack.
3. One bounded scheduling, diversion, or service-delivery candidate —
   implemented as the held H.R. 1702 district-court capacity envelope.
4. District/case-type comparison baseline — implemented for Texas Southern
   immigration felony-defendant workload; rights and causal measures remain
   null.
5. Cost, capacity, disparate-effect, and reintegration audit.
6. Independently reviewed held Taxlane pack.

## Current semantic boundary

The bounded program now demonstrates scenarios, rights-bounded realization,
transition/security accounting, constrained alternatives, stakeholder
incidence, enactment/appropriation/appointment/staffing/facility delivery
gates, adaptive successors, illustrative normalized comparison, and an
integrated held handoff. Candidate-specific rights and outcome evidence and a
public normalized comparator remain evidence work.

## Non-goals

Person records, prediction, risk scoring, legal advice, detention, sentencing,
prosecution, adjudication, booked savings, allocation, or rate changes.
