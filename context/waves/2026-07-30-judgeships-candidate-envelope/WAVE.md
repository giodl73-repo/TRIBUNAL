# Federal judgeships candidate-envelope wave

Outcome: make H.R. 1702's phased district-court capacity and official cost path
replayable without treating more judgeships as a caseflow, rights, safety, or
savings result.

## Delivered

- A dedicated `tribunal-judgeships` feature crate validates the ten fully
  scored FY2026-FY2035 rows and preserves CBO's sub-$0.5M FY2025 notation.
- `candidate-baseline` reconciles $111M direct judge compensation and $283M
  appropriated court operations to $394M total outlays.
- `candidate-held-pack` supplies a bounded JUS candidate with the 65 permanent
  and one temporary judgeships, biennial phase-in, implementation owners,
  current-authority null, and non-additivity rules.
- Outcome, rights-floor, delivery, version, and overlap gaps remain explicit.

Level 1 candidate-envelope acceptance is complete. Level 2 admissibility is
held pending [candidate-role-review.md](reviews/candidate-role-review.md).
